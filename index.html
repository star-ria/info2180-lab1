I'm interested in Dynamic Web Development because so much of what I want to do in cybersecurity depends on understanding how the systems I'm trying to protect actually work. Websites and web applications are one of the most common attack surfaces, and learning how they're built — from the front end down to how they connect with servers and databases — gives me a much clearer picture of where vulnerabilities can creep in.
